Job Description

POSITION SUMMARY

TransMedics is transforming organ transplantation through the Organ Care System (OCS), a breakthrough medical technology platform. Our Software Engineering team develops and supports the digital systems that power device functionality, data management, and user interfaces within a highly regulated medical device environment.

The Software Engineering Intern will support medical device software development activities, including testing, verification, tool validation, and digital application development. This role offers hands-on experience working within a structured software development lifecycle (SDLC) in a regulated industry.

The intern will collaborate with software engineers to support Mac-based development environments and contribute to iOS-based applications using Swift.

Responsibilities

This position is responsible for, but not limited to, the following:

Software Development Support


Assist in development of software features and enhancements for medical device systems
Support digital software development on Mac platforms
Contribute to iOS development projects using Swift
Assist with scripting and automation tasks as needed


Testing & Verification


Support software testing and verification activities
Assist with writing and executing test protocols
Document test results in accordance with quality and regulatory standards
Participate in debugging and issue tracking activities


Tool Evaluation & Validation


Assist with evaluation and validation of software development tools
Support configuration management and version control processes
Contribute to documentation required for regulated software environments


Collaboration & Compliance


Work cross-functionally with Engineering and Quality teams
Follow structured software development and documentation processes
Comply with all safety and regulatory requirements


What You’ll Learn


Medical device software development lifecycle (SDLC) in a regulated environment
Principles of software verification and validation (V&V)
Exposure to quality systems and documentation controls
Practical application of Swift and Mac-based development tools
Cross-functional collaboration within engineering teams


Management Responsibilities

This position does not have direct reports.

PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTES


General office environment where there is a significant amount of time at a desk, working on computers and reviewing documents. This requires the ability to sit for extended periods. They also may need to stand for meetings or when moving around the office.


Qualifications

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS


Pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or related field
College Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate Student
Coursework in software programming or related technical disciplines
Strong programming and scripting skills
Experience with Mac or iOS platform-based development preferred
Familiarity with Swift programming language a plus
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Effective written and verbal communication skills


Why This Internship Stands Out

You will gain exposure to medical device software development, contribute to real verification activities, and build experience in regulated engineering environments while strengthening your Mac and iOS development skills.
